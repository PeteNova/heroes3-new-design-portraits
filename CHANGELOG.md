# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-29)

- Single mod with 13 selectable faction submods, replacing the 13 standalone v1 packs (206 heroes total).
- Base-faction and special/campaign submods enable by default; Cove, Factory and Bulwark stay off
  until their HotA modules (`hota.cove`, `hota.factory`, `hota.bulwark`) are present.
- Declared conflicts with `portraits-packs` and `ensrick-portraits` on overlapping submods, and with
  the legacy standalone New Design packs.
- Small portraits carry the exact 1-pixel original-style border template supplied by misiokles
  (provenance in `docs/border-source.json`) at 1x/2x/3x/4x.
- All 824 large portrait PNGs preserved byte-for-byte from the September release.

## 1.x (2026-09-25)

- 13 standalone faction packs released as `heroes3-new-design-portraits-<faction>-v1`
  (release `portraits-2026-09-25`, superseded by 2.0.0).
